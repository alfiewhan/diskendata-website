DISKENDATA - Nightlife and private event photography.

At DISKENDATA, we believe capturing the candid moments of a night is important. With years of experience in photography and a passion that runs in the bloodline, I aim to bring every brand and event a collection of photographs that captures the real moments of fun, energy and friendship that happen on the dance floor.

You create the energy, we capture it.
