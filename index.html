ABOUT

DISKENDATA - Nightlife and private event photography.

At DISKENDATA, we believe capturing the candid moments of a night is important. We focus on showcasing energetic crowds, spontaneous moments and the atmosphere that makes each event unique, making sure every photograph feels busy with life and energy, and reflects exactly what your event creates, from small intimate clubs to large-scale club spaces.

With years of experience in photography and a passion that runs in the bloodline, I aim to bring every brand and event a collection of photographs that captures the real moments of fun, energy and friendship that happen on the dance floor.

You create the energy, we capture it.

Thank you for visiting this website.

Alfie Nichols
(DISKENDATA)

BEHIND THE CAMERA

Alfie Nichols

I have been going to events across the country for years. I love getting involved with the crowds, experiencing different events and visiting amazing venue spaces.

I DJ and produce music, and after being part of events for so many years, I decided to start a nightlife and event photography business so I could spend more time doing what I love - my passion, photography.

I started photography at the age of 14 and come from a family of photographers. My mother, who ran Photogenic, worked with celebrities across London and became very successful with her own photography business. That passion found its way into my own life after spending much of my childhood in front of a camera lens.

Photographer and founder of DISKENDATA.
